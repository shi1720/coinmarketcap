# Runway Guard voiceover

Speaker: Shivam Gupta. About two minutes at 145 words per minute. Read the numbered sentences in order. Numbers and action notes are not spoken. Each numbered paragraph can be a separate caption.

## Spoken script

1. Hi, I'm Shivam Gupta. This is Runway Guard, a tool that asks whether your crypto treasury can still pay your team.

2. A portfolio balance tells you what your assets are worth today. It does not tell you whether the next payroll payment is covered if prices fall or funds become inaccessible.

3. Here is a fictional sample team. It has crypto holdings, twenty-five thousand dollars in fiat cash, and forty-two thousand dollars in monthly operating costs.

4. I can enter balances manually or import a CSV, then schedule payroll and add one-off expenses.

5. Refreshing fetches real CoinMarketCap quotes. The evidence view shows the response and timestamps behind the calculation.

6. Now I apply a forty percent decline to volatile assets and a ten percent USDC depeg. These are assumptions, not predictions.

7. The chart compares current and stressed coverage as expenses come due. Locked assets cannot fund those expenses.

8. A recurring payroll row schedules its share of monthly burn. The app does not count that expense twice.

9. I can also simulate losing access to an asset and see how the available funds change.

10. The reserve what-if estimates how much fiat we need for our chosen reserve period, and which available volatile holdings could fill the gap under these assumptions.

11. This is a planning estimate. The app does not execute trades, and reported market volume does not guarantee sale proceeds.

12. A saved report preserves the inputs, quotes and assumptions so someone else can review the result after prices change.

13. The commercial hypothesis is a focused tool for small crypto-native teams. The next step is to validate a weekly planning workflow with those teams, then add paid reviews and alerts.

14. Runway Guard connects a changing market to a concrete question: can we cover the obligations coming due?

## Screen actions

- Sentences 1 and 2: Product heading and readiness summary.
- Sentences 3 and 4: Sample holdings, fiat cash, monthly burn and obligation editor.
- Sentence 5: Refresh, then the real API evidence view.
- Sentences 6 and 7: Stress settings and current/stressed chart.
- Sentence 8: A recurring payroll row within the total monthly burn.
- Sentence 9: Asset access freeze and availability warning.
- Sentences 10 and 11: Reserve estimate with assumptions visible.
- Sentence 12: A previously generated report, JSON export or print view.
- Sentences 13 and 14: Return to the summary and hold for two seconds.
